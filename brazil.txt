TITLE
Why Brazil Speaks Portuguese 🇧🇷🇵🇹 The Line That Split the World 🤯

DESCRIPTION
Brazil 🇧🇷 is the only country in the Americas that speaks Portuguese, while most of its neighbors speak Spanish 🇪🇸 How did Portugal end up with half of South America? It started in 1492 when Columbus sailed west for Spain ⛵ In 1493 Pope Alexander VI drew a line down the Atlantic: everything west of it for Spain. Portugal protested, and in 1494 the Treaty of Tordesillas moved the line further west 📜 In 1500 Pedro Álvares Cabral's fleet, on its way to India, landed on a coast east of that line, inside Portugal's half. Later, explorers from São Paulo pushed far beyond the line, and in 1750 the Treaty of Madrid let Portugal keep those lands 🗺️ Today around 200 million people in South America speak Portuguese 🤯
