TITLE
Why 95% of Egyptians Live on 5% of Egypt 🇪🇬🤯 The Nile Explained 🌊

DESCRIPTION
Egypt 🇪🇬 is home to more than 107 million people, but about 95% of them live on just 5% of the country's land: the Nile Valley and the Delta 🌊 The rest is desert 🏜️ South of Cairo it almost never rains, and Cairo itself gets only about 25 mm of rain a year ☀️ For thousands of years the Nile's yearly flood left rich soil along its banks and made Ancient Egypt possible 🌾 In 1970 the Aswan High Dam tamed the floods. Today Egypt still gets about 90% of its water from the Nile, which is why the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam, 2,500 km upstream and opened in 2025, worries Egypt so much ⚠️
