Why Canadians Live Near the US Border 🇨🇦🤯 Toronto Is South of Seattle?!

DESCRIPTION
Canada is the second biggest country in the world 🇨🇦 but almost nobody lives in most of it. Two out of three Canadians live within 100 km of the US border 🇺🇸, on only about 4% of Canada's land. Even crazier: Toronto, Montreal and Ottawa are all further south than Seattle 🤯 Why? About half of Canada has permafrost ❄️, the Canadian Shield is mostly thin soil and bare rock 🪨, and more than 60% of Canadians live along the Great Lakes and the St. Lawrence River between Windsor and Quebec City. Meanwhile Nunavut, a territory almost as big as Mexico, has fewer than 40,000 people.
