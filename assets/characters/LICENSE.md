# Character art

Flat 2D cartoon characters generated locally with Stability AI **SDXL-Turbo** (4 steps, CPU),
background removed with rembg (isnet-general-use), main figure kept, cropped to 700 px height.
Prompt + seed for each character: `prompts/<id>.json`. Each image was reviewed by eye: single
figure, no extra people or stray props, period-appropriate and modest clothing.

SDXL-Turbo is released under the Stability AI Community License (commercial use allowed for
individuals and organisations under USD 1M annual revenue).
